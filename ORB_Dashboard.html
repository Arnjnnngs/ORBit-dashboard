How to use ORBit Dashboard
Quick start — 1 minute, once per device
  1. Click Connect data (top right).
  2. Click Get your free key — it opens Twelve Data. Create a free account (no card needed) and copy your API key.
  3. Paste it and click Test & Save. It's stored on this device, so you won't be asked again.
Each person uses their own free key, so nobody shares limits.
Using the dashboard
Chart — a live TradingView chart of the selected symbol. Click a ticker chip to switch, or type a ticker and press + Add.
Scanner — your whole watchlist at a glance: price, % change, ORB bias, the opening range, Fib levels, and status (Inside / Breakout / Failed). Use OR Window to set the opening-range length (5, 15, or 30 min). Refresh pulls fresh data.
Alerts — a running feed of breakouts, breakdowns, and bias failures. Filter by type or clear them.
P&L Tracker — trades log automatically on a breakout (or fire one manually from the Chart panel). Hit Close with the exit price to record the result; the KPIs update automatically.
FAQ & troubleshooting
It says “Market closed,” or prices show a “last close” tag.
The US market is closed (regular hours 9:30 AM–4:00 PM ET, Mon–Fri). You're seeing the last session's data; live updates resume automatically when it reopens.
A row says “Awaiting opening range.”
The opening range hasn't finished forming. It completes a few minutes after 9:30 AM ET (after your chosen 5/15/30-min window). Bias and levels appear once it's set.
A ticker shows “Invalid symbol.”
That symbol wasn't found on a US exchange — check the spelling. For class shares use a dot, e.g. BRK.B.
Updates paused, or “hit the free data limit.”
The free data plan has a daily cap. The dashboard automatically slows down and resumes, and only polls while the market is open to conserve usage. If it happens often, make sure each person uses their own key, or upgrade your Twelve Data plan.
“Your data key was rejected.”
Re-copy the whole key (no extra spaces) and reconnect via Connect data at the top right.
Nothing loads.
Check your internet, and make sure you've connected a key — the top right shows “Data connected” when you're set.
Do I need an account or password?
No. Your key and watchlist are saved in this browser. On a different device or browser you'll re-enter the key once. (Cross-device sync may come later.)
Is my key safe?
Yes — it's stored only on your device and sent only to Twelve Data (the data provider). It never goes anywhere else.
The numbers differ slightly from my broker.
Free market data can be lightly delayed, and the opening range is built from 5-minute bars. Always confirm exact fills in your broker.
About the data & your API key
Prices come from Twelve Data's free tier (about 800 requests/day, 8/minute). To stay inside that, the dashboard locks each symbol's opening range once it's set, then only checks the live price about every 30 seconds while the market is open.
Get a free key at twelvedata.com → sign up → copy the API key → paste it via Connect data. You can reopen that screen anytime to change keys. This is an educational tool — not financial advice.
